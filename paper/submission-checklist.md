# NeuroSelect manuscript submission checklist

The journal-neutral manuscript can be assembled before the external gates are complete. Actual
submission remains fail-closed.

## Reproducible files

- [x] Frozen publication protocol and source-manifest pins.
- [x] Clean publication display with ten tables and five figures.
- [x] Full journal-neutral manuscript source.
- [x] Verified bibliography with persistent source URLs.
- [x] Quantitative claim ledger.
- [x] Reproducible DOCX assembler and artifact manifest.
- [x] Tracked LaTeX manuscript and BibTeX bibliography.
- [x] Self-contained LaTeX bundle with vector figures.
- [x] Successful Tectonic compilation.
- [x] Page-by-page DOCX and PDF rendering and visual inspection.
- [ ] Clean post-commit manuscript rebuild without `--allow-dirty`.

## Scientific review

- [ ] Obtain a review from a supervisor or independent BCI researcher.
- [ ] Ask the reviewer to check EEG terminology, participant-level uncertainty, calibration
  interpretation, counterfactual boundaries, and whether the paper's contribution is stated at the
  right strength.
- [ ] Record and resolve reviewer comments without replacing unfavorable frozen outcomes.
- [ ] Run the full repository verification suite after revisions.

## Institutional and author metadata

- [ ] Obtain institutionally approved wording for secondary use of public, deidentified Study P
  data; do not independently assert exemption.
- [ ] Confirm University of Innsbruck affiliation and institutional email.
- [ ] Confirm ORCID.
- [ ] Confirm funding statement.
- [ ] Confirm competing-interest statement.
- [ ] Confirm final CRediT roles.
- [ ] Confirm final authorship. A reviewer becomes an author only after a qualifying intellectual
  contribution and acceptance of responsibility.

## Venue preparation

- [ ] Ask `open-access@uibk.ac.at` whether the primary journal, Original Research article type,
  student corresponding-author status, and remaining Taylor & Francis allocation are eligible.
- [ ] Recheck the primary journal's current author instructions immediately before submission.
- [ ] Adapt title page, abstract length, headings, reference style, figure placement, declarations,
  and supplementary files without changing the evidence hierarchy.
- [ ] Prepare a cover letter that describes the work as offline computational Original Research.
- [ ] Explicitly state that no participant used NeuroSelect and that counterfactual phrases were not
  participant-authored selections.
- [ ] Generate and archive the final clean submission manifest.
