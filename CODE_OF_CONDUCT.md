# Code of conduct

## Our standard

NeuroSelect welcomes constructive participation across neuroscience, machine learning,
accessibility, safety, software engineering, and reproducible research. Be respectful, specific,
and evidence-oriented. Make room for people with different expertise and lived experience,
especially people who use augmentative and alternative communication.

Examples of acceptable participation include:

- criticizing methods or claims without attacking people;
- stating uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest;
- protecting participant, contributor, and reporter privacy;
- using accessible language and responding constructively to correction; and
- preserving the distinction between synthetic, original-task EEG, counterfactual, and live
  evidence.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate misrepresentation of
research evidence, disclosure of private information, and publication of sensitive data or
credentials are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and project
events. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub Security Advisory when confidentiality matters.
If private reporting is unavailable, open a public issue containing only a request for a private
contact channel. Do not place personal data, raw EEG, private messages, or credentials in a report.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is necessary to review and resolve a report. Enforcement
decisions should document the relevant behavior and avoid retaliation against good-faith
reporters.
